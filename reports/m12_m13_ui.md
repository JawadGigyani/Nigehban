# M12 + M13: authority board and citizen app

Built 19 Nov 2026. Next.js 15.5.29 (patched release; 15.5.4 had a known vulnerability), MapLibre 4.7.1,
plain CSS. Code in `frontend/`; new backend routes in `backend/app/api/risk.py`.

## Citizen app (`/`)

- Urdu in Nastaliq, right to left, by default; English and Roman Urdu switch in the header.
- **Risk**: search a village or tehsil (English or Urdu); level for today and the next 3 days in
  colour and words, plain-language reasons, river flag note, nearest safer places and Rescue 1122
  (marked unverified), "call 1122".
- **Alerts**: subscribe to villages or tehsils; alerts appear in the in-app inbox (refreshes every
  minute); optional browser notifications when VAPID keys are set.
- **Ask**: the grounded agent (M8); shows "answer checked" and its sources.
- **My data**: language, demo replay (14 Aug 2025), stop alerts, delete all my data.
- Installable (manifest + service worker; offline shell; push handler). Risk data is never cached.
- Stored on the device: language, chosen places, a random device ID, the subscription ID.

## Authority board (`/board`, English)

- Password login (signed token, 12 hours). `GET /alerts` now needs this token.
- Map of tehsils coloured by level for the chosen day; day switch (today to +3).
- KPIs (tehsils per level), ranked table with the main reason, tehsil detail: days, reasons,
  village exposure counts, high-exposure villages, shelters and higher ground, Rescue 1122,
  river flow, unverified note. Markers for shelters, Rescue 1122 and high-exposure villages.
- Replay switch: live, 14 Aug 2025, 26 Jun 2025, or any date from 1 Mar 2024.
- Alerts sent (live or replay). "Draft advisory" button is in place, disabled until M11.
